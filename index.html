TakeawayThe point where you switch from "eat now" to "wait" is your discount rate. It isn't a fact about the money. It's a fact about the people valuing it. The people who ate and the people who waited looked at the same chocolate and valued it differently.

Every r this semester is this thermometer

Investors' patience, their inflation expectations and their fear of risk add up to one number: the return investors require. Discount future cash at that rate and you get what it's worth today. You'll use it everywhere in this course.
